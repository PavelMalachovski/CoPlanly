## 5. What sharing means here

**Most of what you enter is visible to your linked co-parent.** That is the purpose of the app.
Before you write something, assume they will read it.

- Messages cannot be edited or deleted once sent.
- Ending the link stops future access, but does not un-see what has already been read, and does
  not remove what the other parent has already saved on their own device.
- Deleting your account deletes what **you** entered from our servers, including from your
  co-parent's view of the shared calendar — except the message thread, which your co-parent can
  still read and export for **30 days** before it is deleted (see the privacy policy). It does not
  remove a copy their phone had already downloaded.

**What an export proves, and what it does not.** When you export the record and it is registered,
we keep a fingerprint of the file. We then confirm one fact to anyone who asks: **a file with this
fingerprint was registered with us at this time, for this period.** We do not confirm that what
the file says is true, complete, or what happened — it records what the two of you wrote and
entered, and it says so on its first page. Whether and how a court uses it is for the court.

**It is not an emergency service.** Do not rely on CoPlanly to reach anyone urgently. In an
emergency, call 112.

**It is not a medical record.** The medical fields exist so both parents have the same information
to hand. They are not a clinical record, nobody medically qualified reviews them, and they must
not be used in place of medical advice.
