CoPlanly

The shared calendar for parents who live apart.

The custody schedule, the children’s events, your messages and shared expenses in one place, so both homes work from the same plan.

CoPlanly is an Android app, currently tested with a small group of families.

What it does

  • One custody calendar for both homes Each parent has a colour, so you can see whose day it is at a glance.
  • A schedule that fits your agreement Week on / week off, 2-2-3, 3-4-4-3 or a pattern of your own.
  • Today at a glance Who has the children and when the next handover is.
  • Swap a day, only when you both agree The other parent accepts or declines the request.
  • Messages nobody can rewrite A sent message can’t be edited or deleted.
  • Shared expenses, clearly split Who paid for what, and who owes whom.
  • Everything about the kids in one place Medication, allergies, activities and emergency contacts.
  • A record for your lawyer or mediator A PDF or CSV of what you both recorded and wrote.

Google Calendar, if you connect it

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, CoPlanly asks Google for one permission: to see the events on the calendars you own (calendar.events.owned.readonly). It uses it to copy the events of your primary calendar for the next twelve months into CoPlanly on your phone while the sync is on: when you switch it on or tap to sync, when you open the app and about every fifteen minutes.

  • Imported events stay on your phone. They are marked private, so they are not uploaded to our servers and your co-parent does not see them, unless you open one and make it a shared event yourself.
  • CoPlanly never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google Calendar, and never reads a calendar somebody else shares with you.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Sync, or remove CoPlanly’s access in your Google Account.

CoPlanly’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
